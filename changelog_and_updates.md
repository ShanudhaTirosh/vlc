# VLC Conceptual Redesign Updates

This document outlines the major feature changes and updates proposed in the conceptual redesign of VLC Media Player.

## Playback & Controls
*   **Left-Aligned Play Button:** Kept on the left side to prevent a massive, centralized control bar.
*   **Cone Volume Slider:** Replaced the standard bar with a volume slider shaped like a traffic cone.
*   **Seek Bar Upgrades:** Moved to the absolute bottom, thickened, and equipped with visual hover thumbnails.

## Sidebar Features
*   **Visual Subtitle Sync:** Added a slider-based timeline to visually sync delayed subtitles.
*   **Integrated Subtitle Fetching:** Direct integration with sites like OpenSubtitles.
*   **Show Presets:** Save specific audio/subtitle configurations for specific TV shows.
*   **Live Filters:** Visual preview icons for video effects and audio equalizers.

## Library & Settings
*   **Compact Library:** An overlay library for queuing files without leaving playback.
*   **Unified Settings:** Merged "Simple" and "Advanced" views into clean, scrolling pages.
*   **Visual Editor:** Drag-and-drop customization for the UI toolbar.
