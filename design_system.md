# VLC Design System & Aesthetics

## Brand Identity
*   **Colors:** Retains the iconic VLC orange as an accent color, paired with deep, neutral dark grays and true blacks to modernize the contrast.
*   **Logo:** The classic traffic cone remains the central branding element.

## UI/UX Principles
*   **Shape Language:** Elements retain a slightly rectangular, rigid shape rather than adopting the heavy, bubbly rounding seen in modern web apps. This keeps VLC feeling like a serious, utilitarian tool.
*   **Icons:** Completely redrawn to be mathematically consistent, ditching the outdated two-tone look while retaining familiarity.
*   **Plain English:** Technical jargon in menus (like subtitle auto-detect precision) is replaced with human-readable, descriptive dropdowns.

## Modes
*   **Music Mode:** A dedicated interface focusing on massive album art and audio wave seek bars.
*   **Theming:** Built-in Light, Dark, and Custom Accent color themes accessible from the new onboarding screen.
